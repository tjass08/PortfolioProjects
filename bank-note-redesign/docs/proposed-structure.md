# Proposed Bank Note structure

A starting skeleton to prototype against, running top to bottom from most serious to most positive. Word counts are targets that add up to roughly 900.

1. **Subject line and preheader** — the top item named plainly, plus a second hook in the preheader.
2. **In this issue (about 60 words)** — 4 to 6 one-line teasers that let a banker pick what to read in a few seconds.
3. **The big one (about 150 words)** — the week’s most important news, written Axios-style: what happened, **Why it matters**, one or two supporting bullets, link to go deeper. Replaces the automatic feature article; a feature can still earn this slot when it’s the week’s biggest story.
4. **Quick hits (about 200 words)** — 3 or 4 short regulatory, advocacy or industry items, each a bold lead-in plus two sentences and a link.
5. **By the numbers (about 50 words)** — one stat bankers would repeat, with one line of context. A recurring, recognizable feature.
6. **Coming up (about 100 words)** — events, education and deadlines grouped in one compact list with dates and register links.
7. **People and Places (about 150 words)** — promotions, new roles and M&As as a scannable list, one line per item.
8. **Banking in the Community or Member Spotlight (about 150 words)** — the closing “happy card,” with one photo.
9. **Footer** — feedback link (“What would make Bank Note essential to you?”), preferences and contact.

## Item format (every story)

- **Headline:** what happened or what to do
- **Lede (1 sentence):** the news
- **Why it matters (1 to 2 sentences):** the impact on an Iowa banker
- **Optional:** 1 or 2 bullets of supporting detail
- **Link:** descriptive text to go deeper
