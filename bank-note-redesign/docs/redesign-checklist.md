# Redesign checklist

Run every draft outline, and later every weekly issue, against these criteria. If an item can’t be checked, that’s the next thing to fix.

## Length and selection

- [ ] Issue is about 900 words or less (a three-minute read)
- [ ] 5 to 8 items total, not counting standing member sections
- [ ] Each item was selected, not compressed: we picked the 2 or 3 points bankers need, not a shortened version of everything
- [ ] Nothing is in the issue just because it was in last week’s issue
- [ ] Long content (full regulatory summaries, feature stories) lives on the website, with Bank Note linking out

## The first screen (the 9-second test)

- [ ] Subject line names the single most useful item, not just “Bank Note, Oct. 14”
- [ ] Preheader text adds a second reason to open rather than repeating the subject
- [ ] An “In this issue” list of 4 to 6 one-line items sits above the fold
- [ ] The lead item is the most important news of the week, not automatically a feature
- [ ] A reader who stops after the first screen still knows the week’s top takeaway

## Every item

- [ ] Headline says what happened or what to do (teaches something on its own)
- [ ] First sentence is the news; second answers “Why it matters” for an Iowa banker
- [ ] Body is 1 to 3 short paragraphs or bullets, one idea each
- [ ] Strong words sit at the start or end of sentences; no wordy adverbs or filler
- [ ] One clear link to go deeper, with descriptive link text (not “click here”)
- [ ] Passes the “talker” test: would one banker bring this up with another?

## Scannability and design

- [ ] Recurring bold labels used consistently (e.g., Why it matters, By the numbers, Coming up, Your next step)
- [ ] Clear visual separation between sections so skimmers can jump
- [ ] Visuals or icons break up text where they help, used sparingly and never next to serious news
- [ ] Reads cleanly on a phone and in desktop Outlook (test both before every send)
- [ ] Layout doesn’t depend on images loading to make sense

## Flow and member content

- [ ] Order runs serious to lighter: top news, then regulatory and education, then member sections
- [ ] People and Places, Banking in the Community and Member Spotlight appear in the same spot every week
- [ ] Issue ends on a positive “happy card” (Member Spotlight or Banking in the Community is a natural fit)
- [ ] Events and education promos are grouped, not scattered through the issue

## Audience and accuracy

- [ ] Content passes “Is this important to an Iowa banker, and why?”
- [ ] Any AI-assisted summaries were checked against the original source
- [ ] AP style applied throughout
