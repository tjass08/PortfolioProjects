# Platform options: stay in Constant Contact or move?

As of Oct. 12, 2026. Pricing below comes from third-party software listings, not quotes. Confirm everything with the vendor.

## Recommendation

**Stay in Constant Contact for the redesign.** The problem with Bank Note is length and story selection, not the software. Everything in the new format (scan list, Why it matters, labels, member sections) can be built with Constant Contact’s standard blocks; see `constant-contact-build-guide.md`. Run the new format for 8 to 12 issues, measure clicks, then decide whether a tool would fix a remaining problem.

## Axios HQ

**What it is:** Software built around Smart Brevity. It’s an editor that coaches writers (AI shortening, structure prompts, style checks), plus distribution and reader analytics. It’s marketed mainly for internal and stakeholder communications. Its listed integrations sync audiences from company directories such as Outlook, Gmail, Workday and Okta. Distribution includes email, Slack, Microsoft Teams and SharePoint.

**Price:** Software listings put the starting price at about **$15,000 a year**. Pricing is custom, based on organization size and needs. There’s no free version or free trial. G2 lists one edition with unlimited team members and contacts. No public source says whether $15,000 covers one organization or several, so that has to come from Axios HQ sales.

**The math that matters:** Axios HQ would sit beside Constant Contact, not replace it. Event promos, registration emails and the IBMC and IBIS sends would all stay in Constant Contact. So the comparison isn’t $15,000 against $12,000. It’s $12,000 against roughly **$27,000 or more** a year for all three companies.

**What reviewers say:** Most praise the writing coach and analytics. The most common complaints are price and a steep learning curve. Review counts are small, about a dozen on some sites.

### Questions for Axios HQ if you take a demo

1. Does one contract cover three separate organizations, each with its own audience, branding and reporting?
2. Is it built for an external member list of several thousand, including unsubscribes and CAN-SPAM compliance, or mainly for employee lists?
3. Can it import our Constant Contact lists and keep them in sync, or would we manage contacts in two places?
4. What does the analytics view show that Constant Contact plus UTM-tagged links doesn’t?
5. Will you run a pilot on one newsletter (Bank Note only) at a lower price?

## Ways to get most of the Axios benefit for less

- **Learn the method, not the software.** The Smart Brevity book, plus the free Smart Brevity 101 guide from Axios HQ, cover the writing rules. The redesign checklist already turns them into a weekly standard.
- **Use AI as the writing coach.** Paste a draft item into Claude and ask for a Smart Brevity version against `redesign-checklist.md`, then verify against the source. That covers most of what the Axios HQ editor does, at no added cost.
- **Get section-level analytics from UTM tags.** Tagged links in Google Analytics show which sections earn clicks, which is Axios HQ’s main reporting selling point.

## When to revisit

Reconsider a dedicated tool if, after 8 to 12 redesigned issues:

- Click-to-open hasn’t moved and the issue is still running long, meaning the team needs a stronger writing guardrail than a checklist, or
- Several IBA staff are writing for Bank Note and holding a consistent voice has become the bottleneck.
