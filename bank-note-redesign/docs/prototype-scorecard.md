## Checklist results

**Length and selection:** Pass. 748 words with 5 news items (top story, 2 policy items, By the numbers, From the IBA) plus Coming up and the member sections. The Habitat build and the Bank Security Seminar recap were cut, not shortened.

**First screen:** Pass, pending a real-inbox test. The subject names the top story, the preheader adds three more hooks and “In this issue” sits right under the header.

**Every item:** Mostly pass. Every news item has a headline that stands on its own, a one-sentence lede, a “Why it matters” line and one descriptive link. The “Why it matters” lines are drafts and need a check by the government relations team.

**Scannability and design:** Pass in the mockup. Three recurring labels: **Why it matters**, **Your next step** and **By the numbers**. One emoji, on the happy card only. Still to test: desktop Outlook and images-off rendering in a real Constant Contact build.

**Flow and member content:** Pass. The order runs from federal policy to IBA news, then member sections, then the Photo of the week as the happy card. Events are grouped in Coming up.

**Audience and accuracy:** Needs review. All facts come from the Oct. 8 issue as sent, and the links are placeholders.

## Judgment calls to discuss

1. **Top story.** I picked the Fed restructuring over the crypto kiosk settlement because it affects how Fed-supervised Iowa banks are examined. The kiosk story is the stronger “talker.” Either is defensible.
2. **Photo of the week as the happy card.** The puppy photo beats Member Spotlight as an ending. Spotlight stays just above it.
3. **Leaders in Advocacy** dropped from a 330-word lead feature to about 90 words in From the IBA. The full story belongs on the website, with a link if one exists.
4. **People and Places** moved up, ahead of the community sections, because bank deals are high-interest news and it now gets an “In this issue” line.
