# Measurement plan

Set a baseline from the last 8 to 12 current-format issues before launch, then compare the redesign over its first 8 to 12 issues. Clicks are the headline measure because open rates are inflated by Apple Mail Privacy Protection.

| Metric | Why it matters | Target direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Click-to-open rate | Best single read on whether opened issues are useful; banking and financial services average about 13% | Up from baseline |
| Unique click rate (clicks ÷ delivered) | Unaffected by inflated opens | Up from baseline |
| Clicks by section and position | Shows whether readers reach past the first screen and which recurring sections earn attention | More clicks spread below the lead item |
| Registrations or actions from Bank Note links | The behavioral outcome that matters most for events and education | Up from baseline |
| Unsubscribe rate | Early warning if the new format misses | Flat or down |
| Open rate | Useful only as an aggregate trend, not proof of reading | Hold steady or rise |
| Reader feedback | Fills the gap the research can’t: what Iowa bankers want | 5 to 10 banker conversations before launch, a one-question poll after |

## Test plan

- [ ] Pull baseline click map by section from recent issues
- [ ] Talk with 5 to 10 member bankers: “What would make Bank Note essential to you?”
- [ ] Build one prototype issue from a real week’s content and score it against the checklist
- [ ] Send a prototype to a small internal or friendly-banker group for feedback
- [ ] Launch, then review metrics after 4 and 8 issues

## Data to bring into this repo

Drop exports in `data/` (create it as needed): per-issue sends, unique opens, unique clicks, clicks by link, unsubscribes, device or client mix and send/open times.
